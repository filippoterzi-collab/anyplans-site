here’s what your week could look like

not an algorithm, not a feed. just a normal week in your city, with people going to the same things.

🗺️

any evening

open the map. see what’s happening near you this week.

👀

right after

tap “i’m going” on a class, a festival or a run — or post your own plan in two taps.

💬

before you go

sort out the details in the group chat with the others who are going.

📍

you show up

there’s a host, a place, a time. no awkward — you’re joining something that already exists.

🙌

next week

the plan repeats, and the group stays. thursday is pottery night now.

❤️ hi, i’m filippo. my city is full of things happening — classes, festivals, rides, volunteering — but they’re scattered across whatsapp groups, posters and pages nobody checks. so most weeks i’d end up doing nothing, or doing it alone.

i wanted one map that answers a simple question — “any plans?” — and a way to join what’s already happening, together with others. so i started building anyplans.

filippo, bergamo 🫶
