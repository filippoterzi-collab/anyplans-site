Frequently asked questions

When is Gioco da tavolo & socialità?

Gioco da tavolo & socialità has 22 dates, from 30 September to 21 October: Wednesday 30 September at 16:00, Thursday 1 October at 16:00, Friday 2 October at 16:00, Saturday 3 October at 16:00, Sunday 4 October at 16:00 and Monday 5 October at 16:00 and more.

Where is Gioco da tavolo & socialità?

The meeting point is Biblioteca comunale "Angelo Meli" di Trescore Balneario - Trescore Balneario (Be, Trescore Balneario, in the province of Bergamo; the page has a link to the map.

How much does Gioco da tavolo & socialità cost?

Gioco da tavolo & socialità is free: no ticket needed (Verifica costo e prenotazione sulla scheda della biblioteca).

Who organises Gioco da tavolo & socialità?

It is organised by TRESCORE BALNEARIO. anyplans collects the event and shows you who else is going.

How do I join Gioco da tavolo & socialità?

Sign up on anyplans with your email, open the event and say you're going: you see who organises it and who else is coming, so you don't go alone. You must be 18 or older.
