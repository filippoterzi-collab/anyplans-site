Frequently asked questions

When is Podisti Insonni?

Podisti Insonni has 14 dates, from 24 September to 16 October: Thursday 24 September at 05:30, Friday 25 September at 05:30, Sunday 27 September at 05:30, Tuesday 29 September at 05:30, Thursday 1 October at 05:30 and Friday 2 October at 05:30 and more.

Where is Podisti Insonni?

The meeting point is Parco della Malpensata, Bergamo, in the province of Bergamo; the page has a link to the map.

How much does Podisti Insonni cost?

Podisti Insonni is free: no ticket needed.

Who organises Podisti Insonni?

It is organised by Podisti Insonni, a group that publishes its events on anyplans: on its page you find the other dates and the contacts.

How do I join Podisti Insonni?

Sign up on anyplans with your email, open the event and say you're going: you see who organises it and who else is coming, so you don't go alone. You must be 18 or older.
