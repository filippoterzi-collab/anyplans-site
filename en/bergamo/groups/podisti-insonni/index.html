Podisti Insonni

Bergamo

Podisti Insonni is a run club in Bergamo that goes out on Tuesday at 05:30 from Centro sportivo comunale, via Serio, Zanica, on Thursday at 05:30 from Malpensata, Bergamo, on Friday at 05:30 from Malpensata, Bergamo and on Sunday at 05:30 from Malpensata, Bergamo. Open to everyone, running at an easy pace.

About us

As written by the group, in Italian.
Ci alziamo prestissimo e alle 5:30 corriamo: martedì e giovedì in pista con un allenatore, il venerdì la corsa tranquilla «Mai più sola» dalla Malpensata, aperta a tutti, con un gruppo che corre e uno che cammina. Nati da una mamma con poco tempo e una scommessa. Tessera 35 €.

Frequently asked questions

When does Podisti Insonni run?

Podisti Insonni meets on Tuesday at 05:30 from Centro sportivo comunale, via Serio, Zanica (In pista a Zanica, serve la tessera (35 € l'anno). Venerdì e domenica le uscite aperte a tutti.), on Thursday at 05:30 from Malpensata, Bergamo (In pista · tesseramento 35 € l'anno), on Friday at 05:30 from Malpensata, Bergamo (Di corsa · gratis) and on Sunday at 05:30 from Malpensata, Bergamo (Di corsa · gratis), every week. If they skip a week, they write it on Instagram (@podisti_insonni).

Where is the meeting point of Podisti Insonni?

Centro sportivo comunale, via Serio, Zanica and Malpensata, Bergamo.

How much does it cost to run with Podisti Insonni?

Podisti Insonni asks for a membership or a fee: the details are in the group's description above (in Italian).

Do I need to register to run with Podisti Insonni? I'm only in Bergamo for a few days.

Usually not, and one-off visitors are welcome: show up at the meeting point with running shoes and run with the others, at an easy pace. If it is your first time, let them know on Instagram (@podisti_insonni). On anyplans you can follow the group and see the next outings.
