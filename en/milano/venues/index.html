495: Milano (posto nella chat), FeelMiBike, 6Padel Pogliano Milanese, Gate Milano, MUP - Milano Urban Padel, Santeria, Circolo Magnolia and Beat Curator and more. Each has its own page with the upcoming dates.

I run a venue: how do I get on here?

Publish your dates on anyplans: sign up, create your group and add the events. The page of your venue builds itself from the dates you publish.
