Running on Monday in Milan

Running Rebels Milano at 19:30 from Vetra, via Molino delle Armi, Milano (8-11 km a seconda del ritmo, si corre per 40 minuti). Each run lasts about an hour; the group sets the pace, and nobody is left behind. If it is your first time, write on the club's WhatsApp group or Instagram to know whether they go out that week.

Frequently asked questions

Which run clubs go out on Monday in Milan?

On Monday in Milan and its province 1 run club goes out: Running Rebels Milano at 19:30 from Vetra, via Molino delle Armi, Milano.

What time do they run on Monday in Milan?

At 19:30, with Running Rebels Milano.

No. Show up at the meeting point with running shoes: the pace is easy and nobody is left behind. To know whether the group really goes out that week, write on its WhatsApp group or on Instagram, which you find on the club's page on anyplans.
