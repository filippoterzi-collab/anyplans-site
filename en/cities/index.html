My city is not here. Why?

Because there are not enough events yet to make a page worth reading. The map covers the whole country: open it and drag it where you live, the events load as you go.

Where do the events come from?

From the people and groups who publish them on anyplans, and from public sources we read every day (town councils, venues, clubs and event platforms). Every page links back to the organiser.

How much does it cost?

anyplans is free. When an event has a ticket or a fee, the price is on its page and you pay the organiser, not us.
