last updated: august 11, 2026

what anyplans is

anyplans helps you find people to do sports with: you see activities on a map, propose your own, tap "i'm going", and organise in the group chat. The service is provided by Filippo Terzi (hello@anyplans.in).

requirements

You must be at least 18 years old. One account per person, with real information.

activities are organised by users

The activities you see are proposed by other users or reported from external sources (in that case we say so, with a link to the source). anyplans does not organise the outings, does not verify routes, and is not responsible for what happens during activities: you take part at your own risk. Always assess your own ability, the route, the weather, and the people you are meeting.

rules of conduct

No offensive content, spam, harassment, or dangerous or illegal activities. We may remove content and suspend accounts that break these rules.

content you post

It stays yours. You give us permission to display it in the app (that is how the service works). Activities reported from external sources remain the property of those sources, which we link to.

account deletion

You can delete your account at any time in the app (profile → settings → delete account).

limitation of liability

The service is provided "as is", with no guarantee that it will always be available or error-free. To the extent permitted by law, we are not liable for damages arising from the use of the app or from taking part in activities.

governing law
